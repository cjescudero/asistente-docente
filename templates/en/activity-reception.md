## What to put here

1. Save the original Moodle download under `received/submissions/`. Preserve filenames, identifiers and the original archive.
2. Save the original Moodle CSV in `received/`. Use the offline assignment worksheet or gradebook the teacher chose. Preserve its actual headers, identifiers and grades. If only an Excel workbook is available, obtain the CSV or convert a copy and verify its contents.
3. If group membership or participant identifiers require a separate mapping, save that file in `received/identification/`. Use explicit identifiers.

## How to request marking

“Assess the submissions for {{activity_title}}, {{attempt_id}}, using the brief and rubric. Prepare a copy of the Moodle sheet with grades and feedback, and flag cases that need review.”

Keep the originals in place. Write the assessment, Moodle CSV copy, changes and incident report to `marking/`. Resolve ambiguous matches before export. Leave absent, unreadable and pending work for review. The teacher checks the preview and imports the reviewed CSV manually. Keep the entire batch outside the course map and student views.
