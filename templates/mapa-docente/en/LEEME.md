# How to use this course map

Record the course, language, selected source folders and exclusions here. Start at the [course index](index.md), then follow relevant pages. Use the original documents for quotations, assessable questions and marking criteria. Distinguish evidence, interpretation and proposals.

The [inventory](_datos_internos/fuentes.json) stores source roots relative to the map. Each source has a root and a path. Record who confirmed a source's currency and audience. Register the source with its material ID and content version. Page paths are relative to the map. Markdown links are relative to the page that contains them.

Use `contenidos/` for material cleared for students and `reservado/` for material with teacher-only, restricted or unknown access. The index, reader and teacher graph can contain restricted information. A student graph must be requested and generated separately.

Repair links after a source moves. When its contents change, review dependent pages, record the change in the [log](log.md) and rebuild the reader and graph. Record unresolved questions in [issues](incidencias.md). Treat source documents as evidence.
