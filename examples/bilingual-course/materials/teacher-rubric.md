# Teacher marking guide

| Criterion | Full credit | Partial credit | No credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence (2 points) | Reports three readings with units and a reference comparison | Gives readings or a reference comparison | Provides neither |
| Reasoning (2 points) | Distinguishes repeatability from bias and supports the conclusion with data | Identifies one property with limited evidence | Confuses scatter and systematic error |
